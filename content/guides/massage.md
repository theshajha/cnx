## About Massage in Chiang Mai

Thai massage here ranges from 200 THB street chairs to full spa journeys. Locator-style directories list hundreds of shops; this shortlist favors hygiene, communication, and repeat visits. If anything feels off, leave — there is always another chair down the soi.
