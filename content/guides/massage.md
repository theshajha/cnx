## About Massage in Chiang Mai

Thai massage in Chiang Mai is both world-class and incredibly affordable. A 1-hour Thai massage typically costs 200-300 THB ($6-9 USD) at local spots. The quality varies enormously — tourist-area shops can be hit or miss, while the places listed here are consistently good.
