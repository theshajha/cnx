## About motorbikes in Chiang Mai

Directories like Chiang Mai Locator list dozens of renters; quality still comes down to the individual bike ID. Always wear a proper helmet, photo the vehicle in daylight, and keep a copy of the rental agreement. International Driving Permit rules apply — this is not legal advice.
