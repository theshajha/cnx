## About Coffee in Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai's café culture is world-class. Northern Thailand grows arabica around Doi Suthep and Doi Inthanon, and the city has hundreds of independents. For remote work, prioritize WiFi, outlets, and tolerance for laptops — the picks above mix Locator-scale breadth with what long-stay residents actually return to.
