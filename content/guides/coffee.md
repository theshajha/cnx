## About Coffee in Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai's café culture is world-class. Northern Thailand grows its own arabica beans in the hills around Doi Suthep and Doi Inthanon, and the city has embraced specialty coffee with hundreds of independent cafés.

For remote workers, the key factors are WiFi speed, power outlets, and how long you can camp out without getting side-eye. The spots listed here are all nomad-tested.
