## Overview

Smith Suites is the upscale sibling of Smith Residence in the same Chiang Mai Gate area, with 46 rooms and full kitchens in every unit. A rooftop pool, fitness center, and cable TV (CNN, BBC, Fox Movies, AXN) are included. Same Saturday Walking Street doorstep location and 5-minute walk to Old City.

## Expat Tips

- Full kitchen in every room makes this one of the most practical serviced apartments near Old City
- Same location advantage as Smith Residence but with better fit-out and kitchen
- 46 rooms means a tighter-knit community than large condo towers

## Gotchas

- Cash only — no credit cards accepted whatsoever
- 1 month security deposit plus 1 month advance, plus 3,000 THB reservation deposit — read the fine print
- Extra cleaning at 300 THB/session; not included in base rate
- Electric at 8 THB/unit is above average
