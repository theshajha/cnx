## Overview

Doi Ping Mansion is a 12-floor, 120-unit condo built in 1994 on Charoen Prathet Road, one block from the Ping River. Facing Wat Chai Mongkol temple and next to Alliance Francaise, it has a real communal vibe with a mix of retirees and Thai residents. 1BR at 9,000 THB (12-month lease) is excellent value for a riverside condo with pool and gym.

## Expat Tips

- 9,000 THB for a 64sqm 1BR with pool and gym near the Ping River is exceptional value
- Request east-facing units for Ping River views and to face Wat Chai Mongkol
- Long-term resident community gives this a different feel from purely nomad-focused condos
- Alliance Francaise next door — useful for French speakers and language classes

## Gotchas

- Built in 1994 — old building; unit quality varies significantly by owner
- 6-month lease adds 1,000 THB/month premium over the 12-month rate
- No included WiFi; Sinet fiber available to arrange in-unit
- Electric and water rates are owner-dependent; confirm at signing
