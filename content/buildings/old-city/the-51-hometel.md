## Overview

The 51 Hometel is a sleek black-and-white styled serviced apartment/hotel hybrid in Chang Phueak. A digital nomad favorite thanks to the free co-working space on the ground floor and free bicycles for exploring. Water is included in rent (unusual and saves money), plus weekly housekeeping. Some rooms have kitchens and bathtubs.

## Expat Tips

- Free co-working space on the ground floor is a significant differentiator for remote workers
- Free bicycles for exploring the city — saves on daily Grab costs
- Water included in rent is rare and meaningfully reduces monthly costs
- Weekly housekeeping included at all price points

## Gotchas

- No pool and no gym
- Location in Chang Phueak is "a bit far" from Old City and Nimman per reviews; plan on using the bicycle or Grab daily
- Not all rooms have kitchens or bathtubs — specify your preference when booking
