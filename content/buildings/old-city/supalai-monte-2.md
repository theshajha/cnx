## Overview

Supalai Monte 2 is the tallest condo near the Old City area, with 32 floors and 734 units built in 2018. Walking distance to Central Festival Chiang Mai and Warorot Market, and minutes from Tha Phae Gate. Supalai is a reputable Thai developer with consistent build quality. Corner units on high floors have Doi Suthep views.

## Expat Tips

- Upper floors (15+) offer genuinely dramatic views — Doi Suthep views from corner units are a highlight
- Walking distance to Central Festival and Warorot Market for shopping and fresh produce
- Modern 2018 build means better construction and fixtures than older condos in the area

## Gotchas

- Near Tha Phae / east side of Old City — not in Nimman; different lifestyle and commute
- 734 units means you need to specifically vet your unit quality; it varies
- Electric and water rates are owner-dependent — confirm before signing
