## Overview

View Doi Mansion is Santitham's best-known budget apartment on Morakot Road. Standard rooms start at 5,000 THB/month — among the cheapest livable options near Nimman. Higher floors have stunning Doi Suthep mountain and city views. Two desks per room makes it unexpectedly good for remote work. Water is billed at a flat 200 THB/month.

## Expat Tips

- 5,000 THB/month is genuinely hard to beat in this area — great for extended budget stays
- Upper floors have Doi Suthep views that rival apartments costing 3x more
- Two desks per room is an unusual setup that works well for couples working remotely
- Walk to Tesco Lotus Express on Santitham Road for daily groceries

## Gotchas

- No pool and no gym — minimal facilities for the price
- Standard rooms at 28sqm are small; fine for solo nomads, cramped for couples
- 10 minutes walk to Kad Suan Kaew mall; 10-15 min walk to Nimman proper
