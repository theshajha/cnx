## Overview

Srithana Condominium comes in two buildings: Srithana 1 (9 floors, 135 units, ~30 years old) and Srithana 2 (15 floors, 320 units, newer). Both are on Huay Kaew Road, 500m from Nimman. Studios from 6,800 THB make this one of the cheapest condos walkable to Nimman. Srithana 2 is significantly better with a larger parking structure and better lobby.

## Expat Tips

- Srithana 2 is the clear choice over Srithana 1 — newer build, better parking, cleaner lobby
- 500m from Nimman Road — genuinely walkable to cafes and coworking
- Mango Bikes rental on the ground floor of Srithana 2 — handy for getting around
- Studios from 6,800 THB is one of the best price-to-location ratios near Nimman

## Gotchas

- No pool and no gym at either building — significant drawback vs newer condos at similar prices
- Srithana 1 is old and it shows; stick to Srithana 2 unless the price difference is large
- WiFi self-arranged: 3BB or TrueOnline for Srithana 1, Sinet for Srithana 2
- Juristic office hours are limited (weekdays until 4:30-5:30); plan admin tasks accordingly
