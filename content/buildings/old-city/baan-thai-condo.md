## Overview

Baan Thai Condominium is a budget condo on Nimman Soi 6 managed by V.Wanna Asset. Studios from 6,000 THB with pool access and cable TV (60 channels). The pool is described as "huge" and suitable for lap swimming. From expat blog reviews and Foursquare tips (5 tips from 120 visitors), the building is quiet, community-oriented, and popular with nomads and retirees. No online booking system — you must call or show up in person. The building sits directly under the Chiang Mai airport flight path.

## Expat Tips

- Electric at 7 THB/unit is standard for Chiang Mai condos. Water is a flat 200 THB/month
- Pool access at 6,000-9,000 THB/month is the best value pool option near Nimman
- Near Tesco Lotus Nimman for easy grocery shopping
- Two elevators for the building — no long waits

## Gotchas

- Budget building — don't expect premium finishes or services
- The 2BR at 128sqm for 9,000 THB is suspiciously cheap — verify current pricing directly
- WiFi is 600 THB/month extra, not free. Water is a flat 200 THB/month. These add-ons change the budget math
- Under the airport flight path — planes and occasional military jets fly over at low altitude
- Not directly inside Old City; a short tuk-tuk or Grab ride to the moat
- Contact via LINE or email for best response; phone lines may be inconsistent
