## Overview

Smith Residence is a 77-unit serviced apartment near Chiang Mai Gate with a rooftop pool offering Doi Suthep views. Walking distance to Saturday Night Walking Street (Wualai) and 5 minutes to Old City through Chiang Mai Gate. One of the best-value rooftop pool options in the Old City area, with studios from 8,000 THB.

## Expat Tips

- Saturday Night Walking Street is literally on your doorstep — great for food and culture immersion
- Rooftop pool with Doi Suthep views is a genuine highlight; request upper floors for the best view angle
- Good-value base for Old City access without Nimman pricing

## Gotchas

- Cash-preferred payment — bring THB; credit cards may not be accepted
- Water billed at 250 THB minimum for first 5 cubic metres, then 30 THB/cubic metre
- Extra cleaning costs 300 THB/session — not included
- Electric at 8 THB/unit is on the higher end
