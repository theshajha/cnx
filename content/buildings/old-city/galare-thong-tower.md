## Overview

Galare Thong Tower is a 15-floor, 248-unit condo on Chang Klan Road, steps from the Night Bazaar and Ping River. Individual owners means significant unit-to-unit variation, with some fully remodeled and others dated. Managed units include twice-weekly cleaning with linen changes. One of the few high-rises in the Night Bazaar corridor with genuine budget options from 6,800 THB.

## Expat Tips

- West-facing upper floors (above 8F) have 180-degree skyline views — worth specifying when inquiring
- Inspecting the specific unit before signing is non-negotiable; quality ranges from renovated to moldy
- Great base for Night Bazaar and Ping River walks; different vibe from Nimman

## Gotchas

- Individual owners = huge variance; some TripAdvisor reviews warn about mold and dated bathrooms
- No pool and no gym — bare-bones communal facilities
- 1 month deposit is the stated norm but verify with each owner; some ask for more
