## Overview

Serene Teak Boutique Apartment is a low-rise property on Taewarit Road in Chang Phueak/Santitham with handmade teak furniture throughout. A lush tropical garden, pool with panoramic Doi Suthep views, and dedicated WiFi router per room set it apart from standard condos. Weekly cleaning and linen changes included. Superior and Deluxe rooms have full kitchens.

## Expat Tips

- 8,500 THB for a pool apartment with Doi Suthep views and weekly cleaning is outstanding value
- Individual Sinet router per room means no shared WiFi congestion — excellent for remote work
- Contemporary Lanna teak design creates a genuinely pleasant home-like atmosphere
- Quiet Thai residential area — very different vibe from Nimman tourist strip

## Gotchas

- Low-rise with no elevator — not suitable if you have mobility concerns or heavy luggage
- No gym; facilities are pool and garden only
- Kitchen only available in Superior and Deluxe rooms — standard rooms have fridge only
