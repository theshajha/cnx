## Overview

Pansook High Quality Condo is a serviced condo on Soi Ratchaphruek off Huay Kaew Road in Santitham, next to Chiang Mai Lodge. All rooms have full kitchens with cooking tools. Mountain (Doi Suthep) views from most rooms. The notable claim is 5 THB/unit electric — significantly below the 7-8 THB norm, though this should be verified directly.

## Expat Tips

- Electric at 5 THB/unit (if accurate) is among the cheapest found — meaningful savings for heavy AC users
- Full kitchen in every room reduces dining-out costs for longer stays
- Quiet soi next to Chiang Mai Lodge; less foot traffic than main Huay Kaew corridor

## Gotchas

- No pool and no gym — you're paying for apartment quality and location, not facilities
- Only 2 reviews at time of research (4.75/5) — limited data; verify current conditions directly
- Prices listed are converted from USD listings; confirm THB rates directly
- Water at 32 THB/unit is above average
