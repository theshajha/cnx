## Overview

Huay Kaew Residence is widely cited as the cheapest property with a pool in central Chiang Mai, with studios from 3,500 THB/month. A long-standing property with decades of operation, it has a pool, fitness center, restaurant, and massage service. 5 minutes from Nimman, 15 minutes from the airport. 1BR suites include full kitchens.

## Expat Tips

- 3,500 THB with pool access is exceptional — hardest value to beat in all of Chiang Mai
- Weekly cleaning is included even in the cheapest rooms
- 1BR suite rooms have full kitchens — step up from the basic studios if budget allows
- Near Kad Suan Kaew mall for shopping and daily errands

## Gotchas

- WiFi is 300 THB/month extra — not included like most competitors; factor into the real monthly cost
- Basic studio furnishing is minimal; the cheaper rooms are genuinely spartan
- Keycard deposit of 200 THB non-refundable at check-in
- Stays under 3 months: 1 month deposit. Over 3 months: 2 months deposit
