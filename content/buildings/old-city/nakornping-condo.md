## Overview

Nakornping Condominium is a 16-floor, 300-unit condo in Chang Phueak built in 2012. With pool, gym, restaurant, concierge, and 24hr security, it punches above its weight for the price. 300 units means good availability and room to negotiate. City views from upper floors are a genuine highlight.

## Expat Tips

- 300 units = always something available; use this leverage to negotiate on price
- Upper floors (10+) have excellent city views — worth paying the small floor premium
- On-site restaurant is useful for days when you don't want to cook or venture out

## Gotchas

- Electric and water rates not centrally published — confirm with each owner directly
- Chang Phueak is a local area; you need a scooter or Grab for Nimman and Old City access
- Building WiFi available but speed/reliability varies; arrange dedicated ISP for work
