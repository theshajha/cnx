## Overview

Promt Condo is a small 70-unit, 7-floor building on Chet Yot Road in Chang Phueak, built in 2016. Just 3 minutes walk from Maya Mall, it has a pool with waterfall features, a mini-gym, and underground parking. The unique sliding-wall design separates the bedroom from the living area in all units.

## Expat Tips

- 3-minute walk to Maya Mall is genuinely useful for grocery runs and daily errands
- Pool with waterfall feature is above average for a 70-unit building
- Sliding-wall room divider creates a flexible open/closed bedroom layout
- Small building (70 units) means a quieter community — you'll likely know your neighbors

## Gotchas

- Only 1BR units available in 29-39sqm — relatively small footprint
- Front desk is 8am-5pm only; outside those hours you're on your own for building issues
- Lobby WiFi available but in-unit Sinet fiber recommended for remote work
