## Overview

Viengping Mansion is a 15-floor, 98-unit condo/serviced apartment on the Chiang Mai-Lampang Road in Chang Phueak, just 200m from Nimman. Full kitchens (fridge, stovetop, microwave, cookware) in every unit are rare at this price point. Pool, sun terrace, free parking, and laundry service. Also listed on Booking.com for short stays.

## Expat Tips

- Full kitchen in every unit makes long-term stays much more cost-effective
- 200m from Nimman Road — essentially walkable to the main strip
- Pool access in Chang Phueak at this price is a strong value proposition

## Gotchas

- Building shows its age; some units feel dated — view before committing
- TripAdvisor average is 3/5 — mixed reviews; unit quality varies
- Electric and water rates not published; confirm at signing
