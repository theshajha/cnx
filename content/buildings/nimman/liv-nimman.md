## Overview

Liv@Nimman is a boutique luxury condo of just 70 units on Nimman Soi 2 by Lucky Living. Built in 2014, it features a lap pool with jacuzzi, gym, sauna, a library, BBQ area, wine bar, rooftop garden, and a 3-storey clubhouse — all in a quiet lane just off the main Nimman strip. Every unit has a washer, full kitchen, wood floors, and full-height windows.

## Expat Tips

- Quiet lane location gives you Nimman access without the noise of the main road
- The 3-storey clubhouse with wine bar and library is exceptional — one of the best in Nimman
- Full kitchens and wood floors make this feel more like a home than a serviced condo

## Gotchas

- Only 15 parking spots for 70 units — car owners need to sort this early
- No 24hr reception — plan accordingly for late-night arrivals or issues
- No pets allowed; confirm with owner even for non-standard arrangements
- Condo fee of 50 THB/sqm applies — clarify if owner covers it
