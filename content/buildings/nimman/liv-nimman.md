## Overview

Liv@Nimman is a 68-unit boutique luxury condo on Nimman Soi 2 by Lucky Living Properties, completed September 2014 across 8 floors. Google rated 4.5/5 from 100 reviews. Perfect Homes calls it "the most extensive assortment of leisure conveniences of any condominium in Chiang Mai" — and the amenity list backs that up: lap pool with integrated jacuzzi, 3-storey standalone clubhouse, gym, sauna, steam room, library, wine bar, BBQ area, rooftop lounge/garden, jogging track, meeting room, and even an EV charger. From a May 2025 Google review: "This is a beautiful complex. Everything here is 1st class. The infinity pool is lovely and clean. The gym, sauna and steam room are fantastic." Residents are almost exclusively foreign — a mix of long-term expats and some Airbnb short-stays. Every unit has a washer, full kitchen, wood laminate floors, and full-height windows. Fiber internet is now available in the building (confirmed Dec 2025 review).

## Expat Tips

- The 3-storey clubhouse with wine bar and library is genuinely exceptional — confirmed operational in current (2025-2026) property listings. No other Nimman condo matches this amenity density at this building size
- Quiet Soi 2 location gives you Nimman access without main road noise. From a Google review: "One block off the main strip so it's quiet." A few minutes walk to One Nimman and Maya Mall
- Full kitchens and wood laminate floors create a home-like feel. "Apartment well equipped" and "rooms are modern, well furnished" per Google reviewers
- Sunset and mountain views from rear-facing (north-facing) apartments confirmed by multiple reviewers. Request north-facing upper floors (5-8) for Doi Suthep views
- Washing machines in each unit — confirmed by reviews. Saves on laundry hassle
- Fiber internet is now available — a Dec 2025 reviewer confirmed: "They now have fibre internet as well, so ignore the previous complaints." Free WiFi in common areas
- Duplex units on floors 7-8 (82-94 sqm) are available at around 40,000 THB/month. Penthouse duplex at 142 sqm is listed for sale at 13.5M THB

## Gotchas

- Only 15 designated car parking spots for 68 units. From Perfect Homes: "a bit limited, but you can normally find some spaces." Motorcycle/bicycle parking is separate. If you have a car, confirm parking availability before signing
- Airplane noise is a real issue. From a Dec 2025 Google review: "The unit vibrates when the plane passes overhead. The jet noise is loud if you open the windows as it's right on the take-off path from CNX." Windows closed helps, but it's something to test during a viewing
- No 24hr reception — office hours only. 24hr security guards are present at the gate, but don't expect someone at a desk at 2 AM
- No pets allowed unless the juristic office grants specific permission. Service dogs excepted
- Pool is "in the shadow" for part of the day per one reviewer. Non-resident access is also a concern: one reviewer wished "they restricted the pool to residents only"
- A Google reviewer (1/5) warned that apartments are "tiny and very poorly designed" for laptop work, calling the work areas in the complex "nice but useless — chairs are rubbish, desks are rubbish." Another reviewer countered with "decent Wi-Fi" and good working conditions. Experiences vary significantly
- Unit condition depends on individual owners. From a review: "Some of the apartments are showing their age, but that's more a function of owners who do/do not maintain them." Always view the actual unit
- Condo fee of ~50 THB/sqm applies — clarify with the owner whether they cover it in the rent
