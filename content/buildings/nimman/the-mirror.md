## Overview

The Mirror is a tiny 25-unit serviced apartment on Sirimangklajarn Road, steps from Nimman. All units have full kitchens and each gets a dedicated WiFi router — not shared building internet. Weekly cleaning and sheet changes are included. Management is consistently praised for responsiveness. Water is billed at a flat 150 THB/month.

## Expat Tips

- Dedicated router per unit (not shared WiFi) is a major win for remote workers
- Weekly cleaning and linen changes included — less to manage day-to-day
- Very responsive management — issues get resolved fast
- Steps from Nimman; you can walk anywhere for food, coffee, and coworking

## Gotchas

- Only 25 units — book early or you will miss it; this is always in demand
- No pool and no gym
- Electric at 8 THB/unit is on the high end
- Cash security deposit required at check-in (cash only)
