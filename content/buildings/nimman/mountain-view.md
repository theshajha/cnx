## Overview

Mountain View is a 54-unit condo by Quality Condo Co. in Chang Phueak, built in 2011. With 8-metre window fronts and 3-metre ceilings, units feel significantly larger than their sqm. The covered pool and gym+sauna are notable for a 54-unit building. Near the mountains for those who want a quieter, more residential feel.

## Expat Tips

- 8-metre window fronts and 3-metre ceilings are exceptional for a mid-rise condo
- Covered pool means you can swim in all weather including rainy season
- Double elevators and 23 sheltered parking spots serve the 54 units well

## Gotchas

- NOT in core Nimman — approximately 5 minutes by car/scooter; need transport for daily life
- Small building (54 units) means limited availability
- Condo fee of 40 THB/sqm/year — confirm with owner whether included
