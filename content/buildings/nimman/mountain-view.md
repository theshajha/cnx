## Overview

Mountain View is a 54-unit condo by Quality Condo Co. (their 3rd project, same developer as The Convention, The Resort, and Mountain Front) on Soi Khiang Doi 2 off Canal Road, built around 2011. The 8-metre window fronts and 3-metre ceilings are confirmed by the developer and multiple property portals — units feel significantly larger than the ~46-49 sqm suggests. Every unit has a balcony with confirmed mountain views: from KaiBaanThai reviews (4/5 from 4 residents): "unimpeded outlook over the green forested foreground to the Thanon Thong Chai mountains" and "views of Doi Suthep from private balconies." On Booking.com, a unit listing scores 9.1/10 from 10 reviews with Facilities at 9.8 and Comfort at 9.5. The covered pool, gym, and sauna are exceptional for a 54-unit building. The developer explicitly designed this for foreign expat residents.

## Expat Tips

- 8-metre frontage + 3-metre ceilings create an "airy" feel that multiple reviewers specifically praise. These are not marketing claims — confirmed across FazWaz, Thailand-Property, and the developer's own site
- Covered pool means you can swim year-round including rainy season. It's described as "small" but free for residents
- Gym and modern sauna room are both free for residents — a generous amenity for 54 units
- Tight security: keycard access, CCTV throughout, 24-hour guards. From a resident review: "The security system here is very strict." Reception staffed 8:30 AM-5 PM Mon-Sat
- Double elevators serve 54 units across floors 2-7. Sheltered parking included
- "Quiet project, modern design, not crowded" — the consistent resident description. Good for people who want peace over action
- Maya Mall is about 7 minutes by scooter. The neighborhood is residential and green — near Doi Suthep-Pui National Park

## Gotchas

- NOT in core Nimman — the soi is quiet and residential, about 5 minutes by scooter to Maya. Walking to Nimman is impractical. You need transport for daily life
- Only 54 units means limited availability. When units come up, they go fast
- The entrance area feels somewhat "deserted" per one reviewer — the soi has very low foot traffic. This is the tradeoff for quiet
- WiFi status is unclear. Building-wide WiFi may or may not be included — verify with management. No source specifically confirms it. Arrange your own ISP (AIS Fibre, True Online) to be safe
- All units are 1BR — no studios or 2BR options. The "Deluxe Corner" in our listings may be a specific corner unit with more space, but most sources describe uniform ~49sqm 1BR units
- Condo fee applies (40 THB/sqm) — confirm with owner whether they cover it in the rent
