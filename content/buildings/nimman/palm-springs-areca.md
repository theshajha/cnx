## Overview

Palm Springs Areca is one of five independently managed buildings in the Palm Springs Nimman complex, developed by CM Supamitr in 2015. Each building has its own pool, fitness centre, twin lifts, and 24/7 security. Areca has 75 units. Won Best Affordable Low-Rise Condominium award.

## Expat Tips

- Each Palm Springs building is independent — Areca's pool and gym are for Areca residents only, keeping crowds down
- Digital locks and lobby WiFi come standard
- 1BR units start at 31sqm — cozy for solo, tight for couples. Visit first.

## Gotchas

- Units are genuinely small at 31sqm — measure your expectations
- Lobby WiFi only; arrange dedicated fiber (3BB, TrueOnline) for serious remote work
- Individual owners set prices — negotiate directly for best rates
