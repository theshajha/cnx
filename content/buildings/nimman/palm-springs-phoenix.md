## Overview

Palm Springs Phoenix is the fifth building in the Palm Springs Nimman complex, located slightly separately on Huay Kaew Road. Same developer (CM Supamitr, 2015-2016), own pool and fitness centre, twin lifts, digital locks, 24/7 security.

## Expat Tips

- Slightly separate location from the main compound gives it a quieter feel
- Don't confuse with "Phoenix Palm Spring" which is a hotel in the same area

## Gotchas

- Same small-unit design as other Palm Springs (35sqm 1BR)
- Lobby WiFi only
