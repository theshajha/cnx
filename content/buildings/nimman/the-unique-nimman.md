## Overview

The Unique at Nimman is a 75-unit condo by Tarina Property on Soi Suk Kasem, just paces from Nimmanhaemin Road, built in 2014 across 8 floors. Contemporary Lanna-style architecture with interiors by Chiang Mai designer Yutthana Mohprasit and modern furnishing by Bangkok's Modernform Group. Described by Perfect Homes as "one of the best-managed buildings" in the Nimman area with "wonderful living spaces." The building has a pool, separate men's and women's steam rooms (a rare find), gym, 2 elevators, and 39 parking spaces. Units are significantly larger than nearby competitors: 1BR ranges from 52-88 sqm, and 2BR from 89-114 sqm. Based on FazWaz data, rental yield runs about 6%, suggesting healthy demand. About 5 minutes walk to both Maya Mall and One Nimman.

## Expat Tips

- 1BR starting at 52sqm is huge by Nimman standards — most newer builds offer 30-45sqm. The largest 1BR here is 88sqm, which is larger than many 2-bedroom units elsewhere
- Steam rooms (separate men's and women's) are genuinely rare in this price bracket. The pool and gym round out the facilities
- Only 75 units creates a real community feel. From property descriptions: you'll know your neighbors, and the building stays quiet
- 5-minute walk to Maya Mall and One Nimman puts you in the heart of Nimman's cafe, restaurant, and coworking scene without living on the noisy main drag
- The Lanna-style design is a genuine differentiator — this doesn't look or feel like a generic Thai condo. Quality of interior finishes is consistently praised
- Its sibling building, The Unique 2 (40 units), is right next door with a sauna, garden, and included WiFi — check both if availability is limited

## Gotchas

- No WiFi included — arrange your own fiber (3BB, AIS, True). Installation takes a few days
- No studio options. Minimum entry is 1BR from 16,000 THB/month. This prices out budget digital nomads
- 39 parking spaces for 75 units could be tight when the building is full
- Limited availability with only 75 units — good-floor units go fast, turnover is low. About 10 rentals listed on Thailand-Property at time of research
- Some units are listed on Agoda for short-term nightly stays (~$27/night), which could mean occasional transient traffic, though the small building limits this
- Electric at 7 THB/unit and water at 25 THB/unit are standard condo markups
