## Overview

One Plus Huay Kaew is part of the 26-building KKN (Karnkanok) chain, with 136 units across 6 floors on Huay Kaew Road. Built in 2014, it offers pool and gym at the lowest price point in the Nimman vicinity. Close to both Nimman and CMU (Chiang Mai University), making it popular with budget-conscious students and nomads.

## Expat Tips

- Budget entry point with pool+gym — hardest combination to find under 10K in this area
- Near CMU campus — good local community feel and access to university facilities
- Part of a large chain; management standards are consistent across buildings

## Gotchas

- 25sqm is extremely tight for a 1BR — this is a single-room studio in practical terms
- No included WiFi — arrange your own ISP
- KKN chain buildings are very no-frills; don't expect premium finishes
