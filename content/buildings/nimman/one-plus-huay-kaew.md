## Overview

One Plus Huay Kaew is part of Karnkanok Property's 26-building One Plus chain — the largest condo developer in Chiang Mai with over 1,700 units collectively. This building has 136 units across 6 floors on Huay Kaew Soi 2, completed February 2014. It's one of the cheapest options in the Nimman vicinity that includes both a pool and gym, plus a sauna. About 500m to Maya Mall, close to CMU campus. Based on Agoda reviews (9.7/10 from 26 guests), short-term visitors rate it very highly — though the review pool for long-term renters is thin. Karnkanok uses cast-in-place construction across all their buildings, which they claim provides better earthquake resistance and sound insulation than prefab alternatives.

## Expat Tips

- Pool, gym, and sauna at the 9,500-13,500 THB price point is the hardest combination to find in the Nimman area. This is the main reason to pick this building
- 500m walk to Maya Mall, near CMU campus — good access to university sports facilities, track, and cheap food stalls
- 24/7 security with keycard access and CCTV is consistent across all One Plus buildings. Management standards are standardized chain-wide
- No building WiFi — arrange your own ISP. Check with management which providers have fiber to the building before signing a lease. AIS Fibre, True Online, and 3BB are the main options (590-1,200 THB/month)
- Common area fee runs approximately 40 THB/sqm/month based on comparable One Plus buildings

## Gotchas

- The "1 bedroom" at 27sqm is a studio with a partition in practical terms. It's genuinely tiny — smaller than many hotel rooms. Fine for one person, very tight for couples
- Pool reportedly goes into shade after 3 PM due to the building's position — an Agoda review noted it becomes "unusable" in the afternoon
- Unit quality varies by individual owner. From reviews: "Studio is spacious enough, but the interior was not the cleanest, with some small cobwebs in the corners." Since each unit is separately owned, maintenance depends on your specific landlord
- No-frills finishes throughout. From reviews: "The price is reasonable but the comfort is really basic." This is budget housing, not a boutique apartment
- The building has almost zero presence on Reddit, NomadList, or expat blogs — it draws more Thai students and local renters than the typical digital nomad crowd
