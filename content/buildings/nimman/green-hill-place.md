## Overview

Green Hill Place is a large serviced apartment complex across three 7-floor buildings on 15,000 sqm of grounds near Super Highway Road. Google rated 3.9/5 from 192 reviews. The standout feature is the pool facility — two saltwater pools (25x20m and 20x15m) with waterfalls, separately rated 4.2/5 on Google from 79 reviews and described as "a great choice if you need to do some serious swimming in Chiang Mai." Badminton courts (weekdays 3-10 PM, weekends 9 AM-6 PM, 120 THB/hour) are a genuine rarity in Chiang Mai rentals. From NomadRental reviews (4.6/5 from 2 reviews): "Really good value for money, and a paradise for digital nomads." The three buildings differ significantly: Building 3 has "completely modern furniture and interior" per a NomadRental reviewer, while Buildings 1 and 2 were described on TripAdvisor as "something out of Stalin's Russia, poorly designed, austere and dark." Building 2 is cheapest (Standards from 10,000 THB), Building 3 is newest.

## Expat Tips

- Building 3 is the one to get. From a NomadRental reviewer who praised it specifically: "completely modern with new furniture and interior, full kitchen (well equipped), a desk/chair along with a great internet connection." Buildings 1 and 2 are older and significantly less appealing
- Two 25-metre saltwater pools with waterfalls are among the best in Chiang Mai for actual swimming. Pool is best in early morning — quiet and empty before swimming classes start
- Badminton courts with organized games (Tues/Fri 1-3 PM, 100 THB) are a genuine differentiator. 120 THB/hour for the court at other times
- Doi Suthep mountain views from north-facing upper floors are confirmed by multiple reviewers and described as "incredible"
- Gardens and grounds are "like a Holiday resort with lush tropical well-maintained gardens" — the 15,000 sqm site has real green space
- Covered parking for ~100 vehicles. 24-hour security with keycard access
- 1 month deposit. Cleaning fee of 500-1,500 THB charged at checkout depending on room type

## Gotchas

- Pool noise is the #1 complaint across all review platforms. Swimming classes with shouting instructors, screaming children, and non-resident visitors (who pay 80 THB entry). About 40% of units face the pool. Morning is the only quiet swim time
- Pool costs extra for residents: 50 THB/visit or 1,000 THB/month unlimited. Most Chiang Mai condos include pool access in rent — paying separately is unusual and adds up
- NOT walkable to Nimman despite marketing claims. From TripAdvisor reviewers: you need transport. Super Highway is not pedestrian-friendly. Maya Mall is walkable but "more comfortable to take a vehicle"
- Buildings 1 and 2 are dated — "austere and dark with inadequate furniture" per TripAdvisor. Insist on Building 3
- Electricity rate is disputed: NomadRental says "government rates," but a TripAdvisor reviewer claims "almost 3x the government rate." Verify in person before signing
- Sauna exists in the pool changing rooms but recent Google reviews report it's broken/shut down. Don't count on it
- Under the Chiang Mai airport flight path — airplane noise noted by reviewers
- Management quality is mixed. Long-term staff praised as helpful; reception and maintenance criticized as "apathetic" and "nearly non-existent" by others
- Hot water issues reported: "medium settings often result in cold showers"
