## Overview

Green Hill Place is a large serviced apartment complex across three 7-floor buildings on 15,000 sqm of grounds near Super Highway. Standout features include badminton courts (rare in Chiang Mai), sauna, BBQ area, and mountain views. Standard rooms start at 10,000 THB — among the cheapest pool options in the greater Nimman area.

## Expat Tips

- Badminton courts are extremely rare in Chiang Mai rental buildings — a genuine differentiator
- Starting at 10,000 THB with pool+gym+sauna is exceptional value, even accounting for location
- Mountain views from north-facing rooms are consistently praised

## Gotchas

- NOT walkable to Nimman — on Super Highway, you need a scooter or Grab for daily life
- Large complex (three buildings, 15,000sqm grounds) can feel impersonal
- Super Highway location has more traffic noise than Nimman sois
