## Overview

Hillside 3 is a large 11-floor V Group condo with 370 units, 100m from Nimman Road on Soi 8. Built in 2009, many units have been renovated and it remains one of the better-value options in the core Nimman area. WiFi is available building-wide plus private broadband options in-unit.

## Expat Tips

- Many units have been individually renovated — view multiple units before committing
- WiFi is included building-wide, but upgrade to in-room fiber for serious remote work
- 10 minutes walk to Maya Mall; good central base without paying Punna-level prices

## Gotchas

- No pool — if you want to swim, look elsewhere
- Noise from nearby bars can reach lower floors on weekends
- Quality varies significantly by unit and owner — inspect carefully
