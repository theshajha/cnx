## Overview

My Hip 2/3/4 is a three-building condo development by Home Development Group (Bangkok-based) in the Chiang Mai Business Park (CBP) area, with 328 units across 7-floor buildings completed in 2014. Each building has 2 elevators and the complex has 240 covered parking spaces. The confirmed standout features are the 3-metre ceilings (verified on the developer's official site) and a saltwater pool (also developer-confirmed). From property review aggregators, the building scores 4.19/5 from 6 reviews, with praise for pool quality, furniture durability, and staff responsiveness. A Booking.com guest noted "very clean, comfortable bed, friendly staff, great value." The CBP area itself is a quiet, well-organized urban village — Big C Extra, Index Living Mall, HomePro, banks, restaurants, and medical facilities are all nearby.

## Expat Tips

- Building 4 has approximately 60% vacancy — this gives you real negotiating leverage on rent. Buildings 2 and 3 have lower vacancy (~20-30%). Target Building 4 for the best deal
- 3-metre ceilings genuinely make a difference. From reviews: units feel larger and airier than the sqm suggests. Particularly noticeable in the 1BR units
- 1BR units start from 10,000 THB based on current listing data — lower than the 12,000 in our original range. Types run from 37sqm (Type A) to 46sqm (Type B1)
- The saltwater pool is described as "wide" and well-maintained in reviews. Facilities overall are "in good condition, taken care of very well"
- CBP neighborhood has everything for daily life within walking distance: Big C Extra (supermarket, night market, food court), HomePro, Index Living Mall, medical clinics, coffee shops, 7-Elevens
- 24-hour security with keycard access, CCTV throughout, fire safety systems. Children's play area on-site — good for families
- Good for families or couples needing space: 2BR at 64-115 sqm, 3BR at 107 sqm at mid-range prices is rare in Chiang Mai

## Gotchas

- NOT in Nimman — this is CBP area, approximately 5-6 km from Nimman by road. 10-15 minutes by scooter in light traffic. Walking is impractical. You need transport
- No building WiFi in units — only lobby WiFi. Arrange your own ISP (AIS Fibre, True Online, 3BB). Confirm which providers serve the building before signing
- No studio options — minimum entry is 1BR. Solo budget travelers should look elsewhere
- Building is showing minor age after 10+ years. One guest reported "nails popping out of the floor." From reviews: "Although it is not the latest project, the overall condition doesn't look old at all" — but inspect your specific unit
- Very few online reviews (6 on property aggregators, zero Reddit threads). The building flies under the radar of the nomad community, likely because it's not in Nimman
- Electric at 7 THB/unit is a standard condo markup over the ~4.5 THB government rate
