## Overview

Hillside 2 sits directly on the main Nimman drag with 228 units across 10 floors. Built in 2007, it's a budget-friendly option with minimal facilities — no pool, no gym — but the location on Nimman Road is unbeatable. There's a 7-Eleven in the lobby. Good for those who prioritize walkability over amenities.

## Expat Tips

- Location is the main selling point — you're literally on Nimman Road
- Budget option: studios from 10,000 THB in a prime spot
- Negotiate hard, especially for 3+ month stays

## Gotchas

- No pool and no gym — bare-bones facilities for the price
- No included WiFi — arrange your own ISP
- Older building (2007); unit condition varies significantly by owner
