## Overview

Yantarasri@Nimman is a serviced apartment building on Soi 8, affiliated with the well-reviewed Yantarasri Resort on Soi 6. All units are 40 sqm studios with modern fit-out. Includes communal kitchen with oven and 6 stovetops, outdoor grill area, and coin laundry. Pool access at the affiliated resort on Soi 6.

## Expat Tips

- Bi-monthly cleaning is included free — rare at this price point
- The communal kitchen is a major perk if you cook — full oven, 6 stovetops, 2 large sinks
- Bathtub in every unit is unusual for this price range in Chiang Mai
- Office hours are 11am-8pm daily — plan viewings accordingly

## Gotchas

- No on-site pool — you must walk to the resort on Soi 6 to use theirs
- No gym on-site
- Electric rate at 8 ฿/unit is on the high side — will add up with heavy AC use
- Aircraft noise — all of Nimman is under the Chiang Mai airport flight path
