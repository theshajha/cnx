## Overview

The Unique at Nimman 2 is the sibling building to the original Unique at Nimman, with only 40 units across 8 floors. Also by Tarina Property and built in 2014, it adds a sauna and garden over the original. At just 40 units it is essentially a boutique residence — availability is rare but quality and privacy are exceptional.

## Expat Tips

- Only 40 units makes this one of the most exclusive non-luxury buildings in Nimman
- Sauna, pool, gym, and garden in a 40-unit building is remarkable value
- WiFi is included — useful as a bridge until you set up your own fiber

## Gotchas

- Availability is extremely limited — you may need to wait or move fast when a unit opens
- No studios — minimum is a 1BR at 47sqm
- Pricing is on the higher end for Nimman condos given the boutique premium
