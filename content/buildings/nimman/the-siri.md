## Overview

The Siri is a modern Lanna-style condo with 59 units across 7 floors plus a rooftop, on Sirimangklajarn Soi 1. Built around 2016, it has a pool with sundeck, gym, sauna, and a rooftop garden with Doi Suthep views. Rated 4.7/5 by residents, it's particularly popular with online teachers and remote workers who appreciate the quiet lane away from core Nimman noise.

## Expat Tips

- Rooftop garden with Doi Suthep views is a standout feature — prioritize higher floors to connect to it
- 6 THB/unit electric is below the Nimman norm — meaningful savings over time
- 50/20 Mbps WiFi is included; upgrade to in-room fiber if you need faster

## Gotchas

- Foreign quota may be at or near 49% limit — if buying, check; not an issue for renting
- Only 1BR units available — no studios
- Water rate of 30 THB/unit is slightly above average; factor into budget
