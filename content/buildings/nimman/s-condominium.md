## Overview

S Condominium is a 48-unit, 7-floor boutique condo on Sirimangklajarn Soi 1 by Pratana Property Co., Ltd. Launched December 2013 and completed July 2015. It received the Thailand Property Award's "Highly Commended Best Residential Development Chiang Mai" — confirmed via DDproperty's coverage of the 2014 Thailand Awards. The building covers about 1 rai of land with contemporary Far East-inspired interiors, a swimming pool (free for residents), gym (free), 100% parking coverage, elevator, CCTV, and 24-hour security. Units are furnished and can be fitted with Sinet fiber-optic broadband on a monthly or annual plan. Located at the end of a lane off Sirimangklajarn's main strip, which is lined with 7-Elevens, coffee shops, and casual eateries, near Makro Food Service and Suandok Park.

## Expat Tips

- The wide unit frontages (described as 9-metre in marketing) create a noticeably more open feel than typical Nimman condos. The 59sqm 1BR here feels significantly larger than a 60sqm unit in a narrower building
- Sinet fiber is the key internet option — installable on monthly or annual plans for fast dedicated broadband. This is a real advantage for remote workers over the shared building WiFi most condos offer
- Walkable to both the Nimman cafe strip and the Sirimangklajarn restaurant scene. Near Makro Food Service for bulk grocery shopping and Suandok Park for running or outdoor exercise
- Pool and gym are free for residents — no additional monthly fee unlike some serviced apartments
- The building is quiet by design: tucked at the end of a soi, only 48 units, no short-term tourist traffic. Based on the building's profile, this attracts long-term residents who value quality over hype

## Gotchas

- Only 48 units means extremely low turnover. Finding a vacant unit requires patience, flexibility on move-in dates, or getting on a waiting list. Don't expect to walk in and find availability
- No studios — the entry level is a 1BR at 59sqm, starting around 13,000 THB. This prices out solo budget travelers
- No pets allowed — confirmed across property listing sources
- Almost zero online reviews exist for this building. No Google reviews aggregation found, no Reddit threads, no NomadList mentions, no TripAdvisor page. This is a building known by word-of-mouth, not by internet presence. The low review volume means you're relying more on in-person visits than online research
- The Thailand Property Award was "Highly Commended" (runner-up category), not the outright winner. Still a meaningful recognition but worth noting the distinction
