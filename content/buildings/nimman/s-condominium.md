## Overview

S Condominium is a boutique 48-unit condo on Sirimangklajarn Soi 1 by Prattana Properties. Built in 2015 and winner of the Thailand Property Award for Best Residential Development in Chiang Mai, it features notably wide 9-metre unit frontages, a saltwater pool, and a walkable location to both Nimman and Sirimangklajarn.

## Expat Tips

- 9-metre-wide units create an unusually open, spacious feeling for a mid-rise building
- Sinet fiber is available in-unit for fast dedicated broadband
- Walkable to both Nimman cafes and the Sirimangklajarn restaurants strip

## Gotchas

- Only 48 units means very low turnover; expect to wait or be flexible
- Common WiFi included but get the in-unit Sinet line for reliable remote work speeds
- No studios — 1BR is the entry level at 58sqm
