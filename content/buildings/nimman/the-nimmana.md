## Overview

The Nimmana is the newest large condo development in core Nimman with three 8-floor blocks and 349 units. Built around 2018, it features a saltwater infinity pool, male and female saunas, a 1,600sqm English Garden, and underground parking for 210 vehicles. Washing machines are standard in all units except studios.

## Expat Tips

- East-facing units get Doi Suthep mountain views without the brutal afternoon heat
- Studios don't come with a washer — factor in laundry costs if you go this route
- The English Garden and infinity pool are genuinely impressive for the price point

## Gotchas

- West-facing units take full afternoon sun and drive up AC bills significantly
- Condo fee of 45 THB/sqm applies — confirm with your owner whether it's included
- Studios lack washing machines; some owners have added portables, so ask
