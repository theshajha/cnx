## Overview

Hillside 4 is Chiang Mai's largest condo complex with approximately 700 units spread across 15 floors. Built around 2005, it has two pools (4th floor and rooftop), underground parking, and an on-site restaurant. It's 5 minutes from Maya Mall and attracts a mix of long-term expats and nomads looking for value in a well-equipped complex.

## Expat Tips

- Nomads rate the 45sqm studios at ~14,000 THB as solid value for the Huay Kaew corridor
- Rooftop pool has better views than the 4F pool — worth requesting a unit near it
- The building is ~20 years old but units vary; many owners have renovated

## Gotchas

- Gym requires a separate paid membership — not free with rent
- Building age shows in common areas; don't expect hotel-standard finishes
- 700 units means turnover is constant — vet your specific unit and owner carefully
