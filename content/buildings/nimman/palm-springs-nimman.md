## Overview

Palm Springs Nimman is a cluster of five buildings (Areca, Fountain, Parlor, Royal, Phoenix) developed by CM Supamitr, each with its own pool and fitness centre. Built in 2015-2016 and awarded Best Affordable Low-Rise Condominium, they offer a self-contained community feel with twin lifts, digital locks, and lobby WiFi. The Royal building is the only one with 2BR units.

## Expat Tips

- Each building is independent with its own pool and gym — you won't be sharing with 500 people
- Mid-range sweet spot: pool+gym combination at the 13,500-15,000 THB price point is hard to beat
- Units are small (31-41sqm for 1BR) — works well for solo nomads, tight for couples

## Gotchas

- 1BR units at 31sqm are genuinely small — visit before committing
- Multiple buildings means management quality can vary between Areca/Fountain/Parlor/Royal/Phoenix
- Lobby WiFi only included; arrange dedicated fiber for serious remote work
