## Overview

The Cosy Huay Kaew is a popular 70-unit serviced apartment on the Nimman/Santitham border, with 7 floors and studios only. It's particularly well-regarded by digital nomads for including water charges and weekly cleaning in the rent. Every studio has a washer, microwave, fridge, kettle, full kitchenette, and balcony.

## Expat Tips

- Water is included in rent — saves budget tracking and avoids surprise bills
- Washer in every unit eliminates the need for coin laundry
- Weekly cleaning included — a genuine time-saver for busy remote workers
- 5th-7th floor units get better views and slightly higher prices; worth the premium

## Gotchas

- Studios only — no 1BR options; couples may find it cramped
- Mountain views are from the higher floors; lower floors look into neighboring buildings
- Confirm current pricing directly — listed rates may have changed
