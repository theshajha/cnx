## Overview

Nivas Chiangmai is a well-reviewed serviced apartment (opened January 2018) with 53 units across 8 floors on Huay Kaew Road. Rated 4.8/5 by residents for its home-like atmosphere and reliable WiFi for video calls. All units have balconies, kitchens, and mountain views. Only 1 month deposit required — lower than most competitors.

## Expat Tips

- Only 1 month deposit is a significant cashflow advantage
- WiFi praised specifically for Skype/video calls with no dropouts — good for remote workers
- Mountain views from north-facing upper floors are consistently rated highly
- 10-15 min walk to Nimman; short Grab ride to anywhere

## Gotchas

- Electric at 8 THB/unit is on the high end — run AC conservatively
- Water at 35 THB/unit is among the highest in the area; 2BR has a 200-300 THB monthly minimum
- NOT in core Nimman — 10-15 min walk; need a scooter or Grab for convenience
