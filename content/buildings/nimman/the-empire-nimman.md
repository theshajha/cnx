## Overview

The Empire Nimman is a former hotel converted to monthly-only serviced apartments with Lanna-inspired architecture. Steps from Maya Mall, it offers Doi Suthep views and includes WiFi, gym access, and an on-site restaurant. All rooms have balconies and smart TVs. Three tiers: Superior (34sqm), Deluxe (47sqm), and Super Deluxe (65sqm).

## Expat Tips

- Steps from Maya Mall — unbeatable convenience for groceries and daily errands
- Doi Suthep views from west-facing rooms are genuinely spectacular at sunset
- On-site restaurant useful for days when you don't want to cook or go out

## Gotchas

- No pool — a significant drawback vs comparable Nimman options at this price
- Superior rooms have microwave only — no real kitchen; budget for eating out
- Converted hotel pricing feels elevated for what you get vs true condos nearby
