## Overview

Palm Springs Royal is the only building in the Palm Springs Nimman complex that offers 2BR units (70-75sqm). With 68 units, it's slightly smaller than the other buildings. Same developer (CM Supamitr), same award-winning quality, own pool and fitness centre.

## Expat Tips

- **Only Palm Springs building with 2BR units** — if you need space for a couple or home office, this is it
- 1BR units here are the largest in the complex at 41sqm
- 2BR at 18,000-22,000 THB with pool+gym is excellent value for couples

## Gotchas

- Higher demand for 2BR units means less negotiation room
- Same lobby-WiFi-only situation — arrange dedicated fiber
