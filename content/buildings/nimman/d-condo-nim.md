## Overview

D Condo Nim is the largest condo complex in the Nimman area, directly on Nimmanhaemin Road. Units are individually owned and rented out — you'll deal with individual owners or agents rather than central management. This means prices and conditions vary widely between units.

## Expat Tips

- Try to find the owner directly — skipping the agent saves both sides the 1-month commission
- Facebook groups "Chiang Mai Rentals" and "Chiang Mai Digital Nomads" often have D Condo listings
- Check multiple units in the building — quality varies dramatically between owners
- The pool and gym are building amenities available to all tenants

## Gotchas

- No central management for rentals — dealing with individual owners means inconsistent experiences
- Some owners charge above-market electric rates — confirm 7 ฿/unit or lower
- Building is large — some units face noisy roads, others face quiet gardens. Visit first.
- Older building — some units haven't been renovated. Always view in person.
