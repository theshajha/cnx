## Overview

Trams 1 is a 166-unit condo by Silver Land House in Jed Yod, built in 2014. At 10,000-14,000 THB for a 1BR with pool, gym, sauna, and garden, it represents strong value for those comfortable being 10 minutes walk from core Nimman. Units are small but the facilities-to-price ratio is hard to beat.

## Expat Tips

- Pool, gym, and sauna at the 10-14K price point is exceptional — rare combination at this budget
- 10-minute walk to Nimman is manageable for those who don't mind the walk
- Good option for longer stays where value matters more than being at the epicentre

## Gotchas

- 1BR units at 25-35sqm are very small — tight for couples; fine for solo nomads
- In Jed Yod, not Nimman proper — factor in the extra commute to cafes and coworking
- No included WiFi — arrange your own ISP from day one
