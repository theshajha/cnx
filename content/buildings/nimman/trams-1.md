## Overview

Trams 1 is a 166-unit condo by Silver Land House across 8 floors in Jed Yod, completed February 2014. Pool, gym, sauna, and garden at 10,000-14,000 THB for a 1BR is the strongest facilities-to-price ratio in the area. Based on property listings, units come fully furnished with two ACs, washing machine, fridge, microwave, rice cooker, and TV — an unusually complete kit at this price point. The building has very few online reviews specific to it (its sister property Trams Square next door gets more attention), but the one verified resident review praises unit size and furniture quality. The building sits between the Superhighway and Canal Road in Jed Yod — walkable to Nimman but not a quick stroll.

## Expat Tips

- Pool, gym, and sauna at the 10-14K price point — the sauna alone makes this unusual. One Plus Jed Yod nearby offers pool and gym at similar prices but no sauna
- 7-Eleven and local market are within 100m of the building. CMU campus is 1 km away — useful for the running track, cheap canteens, and university facilities
- 1BR units range from 30-48 sqm. The larger 47-48 sqm units at 10-12K THB are particularly good value — confirm available sizes when viewing
- 24-hour security with digital keycard and CCTV throughout
- No building WiFi — arrange your own ISP before move-in. Check with management which providers (AIS Fibre, True Online, 3BB) have fiber to the building. This is critical and varies by building
- No pets allowed — confirmed across multiple property listing sources

## Gotchas

- The "10 minute walk to Nimman" claim is optimistic. Based on distance data, it's 1.2-1.5 km to One Nimman/Maya, which is realistically a 15-20 minute walk. Property listings describe it as a "5-minute drive." Budget for a scooter rental if you want easy Nimman access
- Reviews from the sister building Trams Square (same developer, next door) mention: slow elevator, occasional noise from a nearby venue, and inconsistent front desk service. These may apply to Trams 1 as well
- Building is 12 years old — expect some wear on fixtures and finishes depending on how the individual unit owner has maintained things
- Electricity at 7 THB/unit is a ~55% markup over the government rate (~4.5 THB). Not outrageous for a condo, but worth knowing
- The Jed Yod area is noted by some expat sources as being under flight paths — airplane noise possible, though no reviews specifically confirm this for Trams 1
